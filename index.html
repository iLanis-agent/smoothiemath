Smoothie math

Day one is sorbet, day two is juice, and nobody knows what changed. Smoothie math pins the liquid ratio to the texture you actually want, estimates the blender's calories from labeled food data, and portions a week of freezer bags so the morning is grab-and-blend. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Blend balance - frozen fruit and a liquid ratio become the ml to pour, the total, and the glass count.
  • Calorie estimate - banana, berries, yogurt and juice become a labeled kcal estimate per serving.
  • Freezer prep - days and per-day mix become the bags to portion, one per morning if they fit.

Labeled norms

Liquid ratios per 100 g of frozen fruit: under 80 ml spoon-thick, 80-120 classic pour, over 120 easy sipper. Glass size 250 ml. Calorie figures are labeled food-data estimates (banana 89, berries 50, yogurt 60, juice 45 kcal per 100 g) - estimates, not nutrition advice. Your blender and your labels win.
